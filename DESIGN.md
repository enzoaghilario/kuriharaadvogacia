# Design System: Kurihara & Figueiredo

## Overview
An institutional legal identity, not theatrical luxury. Deep blue reading surfaces, graphite section bands, warm pale text and gold reserved for actions and emphasis. Preserve real content, photography and the supplied black logo.

## Colors
Gold distinguishes primary actions and active navigation. Dark blue and graphite establish hierarchy without gradients or decorative panels. Warm light text keeps long explanations legible.

## Typography
Preserve the user's Playfair Display and Manrope pairing. Serif titles carry the intellectual voice; sans-serif descriptions remain direct and readable. Headings are balanced, with no negative tracking or viewport-scaled type.

## Layout
Full-width bands within a 76rem container. Desktop two-column content reflows into a single column. Page introductions use the title directly, without redundant slogan labels.

## Elevation & Depth
Tonal changes and restrained borders organize content. No invented physical materials, bevels or decorative floating cards.

## Shapes
Straight-edged controls and fine rules. The original black logo stays on a light field so its artwork is not recolored or redrawn.

## Components
Gold primary actions use dark text and lighten on hover. Outlined secondary actions use pale text. Keyboard focus uses a gold outline. Service contact buttons exist only in open services. The existing logo entrance and breathing motion remain, with reduced-motion support.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do preserve real photographs and their text.
- Do use shared semantic tokens for all surfaces and states.
- Don't introduce law clichés, invented credentials, promises or decorative value grids.
- Don't canonize existing service numbering or metadata labels as decorative design patterns; they remain legacy content, not a system rule.
