# Product

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Institutional website for Kurihara & Figueiredo Advogados Associados in Morro Agudo, presenting the office, legal services and contact channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Service disclosures open a name-entry dialog and prepare a WhatsApp message for the visitor to review and send. Closed services have no contact button in the DOM. Instagram shows only real stored publications; connection credentials are pending.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the supplied logo, real facade photograph, existing firm content and contacts. Serious, intellectual presentation without fabricated credentials, promises, legal clichés or decorative value grids.
